What is Reiki?

Universal energy, ancient intelligence

Reiki is rooted in a truth every healing tradition has known: that beneath the physical body, we exist as energy — and that this energy responds to everything we experience. Ki (Japanese), Chi (Chinese), Prana (Sanskrit): the same invisible, intelligent life force, by many names. When it flows freely, we feel well. When it stagnates or depletes, we feel it — sometimes as a vague "off" feeling, sometimes as exhaustion, fogginess, or a weight we cannot name. Reiki works at this energetic root, restoring flow, balance, and aliveness from within.

Who I am

Healer, channel, empath

I am a Reiki practitioner and energy healer — a channel for the universal life force that flows through all of creation. My work lives at the intersection of ancient healing wisdom and the very real, very human experience of being alive in a world that asks a great deal of us.

I am highly empathic, Clairvoyant, and Clairsentient. In practice, this means I receive visual impressions and imagery during sessions — and I feel into the emotional and energetic experience of those I work with as though it were my own. I know, firsthand, what it is to feel overwhelmed without knowing precisely why. To be weighed down by something invisible. To feel in need of a deep energetic detox. This understanding lives at the heart of my practice — not as theory, but as lived experience transformed into devoted service.

In every session, I work alongside Spirit: my own guides and ancestors, and the guides and ancestors of each person I serve. Healing in this way is never solitary. It is a gathering — a convergence of love from both sides of the veil — in quiet, powerful service of your restoration.

The energetic world

The living field beneath everything

At the root of all healing — across every tradition, every culture, every era of human wisdom — is a recognition that we are not merely physical beings. We are energy. Living, moving, responsive energy; woven into a vast and intelligent field that connects all things.

In Japan, the life force that animates us is called Ki. In China, Chi. In the Sanskrit tradition, Prana. Whatever name we give it, this current has been known across civilisations and centuries as the invisible force that flows through every living being — and that responds to everything we experience: our thoughts, our emotions, our relationships, our environment, and the stories we carry within us.

Reiki — from the Japanese rei (universal) and ki (life energy) — is the art and practice of consciously working with this field. As a Reiki practitioner, I serve as a conduit: I do not generate healing from within myself, but open as a clear and intentional channel for universal energy to flow, guided always by the highest good of the person I am working with.

I work with sacred Reiki symbols in my practice — ancient geometries of intention that amplify, direct, and deepen the healing energy in specific and profound ways. Each symbol carries its own quality of intelligence; together, they allow the work to reach precisely where it is needed. I also work in active collaboration with Spirit — my own guides and ancestral lineage, and the guides and ancestors of each client I serve. Healing in this way is never done alone. It is a convergence.

The energetic benefits

What unfolds in a session

We are multi-layered beings. Beyond the physical body, we each have an energetic anatomy — a luminous auric field and a system of spinning energy centres called chakras — through which the life force of our lives flows, stagnates, or gets blocked. Everything we experience leaves its mark here: the love we have received and the grief we have carried, the traumas we have survived and the patterns we have quietly inherited. Each session works with what is present and ready to be addressed.

🌀

Chakra Rebalancing & Re-energisation

The seven primary chakras govern different aspects of our physical, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing. When these centres become depleted or blocked, the effects ripple through the entire system — affecting energy, mood, immunity, and sense of self. Reiki gently re-energises and restores each chakra to its natural state of flow and vitality.

✨

Auric Field Clearing

Our aura is the luminous energy field that surrounds and interpenetrates the physical body — our first point of contact with the world. Over time, without clearing, the auric field accumulates energetic residue: old emotional debris, the projections of others, environmental impressions. Sessions work to clear, brighten, and strengthen this vital field, restoring clarity and healthy energetic boundaries.

Trauma & Abuse Imprints

Trauma does not only live in memory — it lives in the energetic body. Unresolved experiences of abuse, loss, shock, or prolonged stress leave their signatures in the field, where they can quietly shape our nervous system, our relationships, and our sense of self for years. Sessions work with compassionate, intelligent intention to soften, clear, and transform these imprints at the energetic root.

Ancestral Trauma Healing

We inherit far more than physical genetics. Patterns of pain, unresolved grief, survival strategies, and emotional wounds can pass through the ancestral line — generation to generation — until someone in the lineage begins to consciously address them. Working with ancestral energy is one of the most far-reaching and quietly revolutionary aspects of this practice, with ripples felt across the lineage in both directions.

The weight we carry
You may not be able to name what you're carrying — but you feel its weight.

We all carry emotional and energetic material that sits just below the surface of conscious awareness — accumulated over this lifetime and beyond. Unconscious patterns, inherited beliefs, absorbed emotions, unprocessed experiences. You may simply feel "off." A persistent, low-level exhaustion that rest alone cannot fix. A fogginess. An inability to make decisions that once came easily. A sense of being weighed down by something invisible and unnameable.

This is often the energetic body asking for attention. Signalling that there are layers ready to be tended, cleared, and released. And it deserves to be heard.

As someone with a naturally open aura who has always absorbed the energies around me — the emotions, the heaviness, the unspoken states of others — I understand this experience from the inside. I know how overwhelming and depleting life can feel when you are carrying not only your own weight, but the weight of everyone around you. I also know how profoundly transformative it is when those layers begin to lift.

One of the things I find most beautiful — and most humbling — about this work is that every person's energetic landscape is entirely unique. As distinct and individual as a fingerprint. There is no template, no formula. When I work with you, I read your specific field and respond to exactly what is present. The session belongs wholly to you.
